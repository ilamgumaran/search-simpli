A big red bus goes down the road. It is late for the stop, so it must
go fast. Kids wait at the curb with their bags and coats on. The sky is
grey and a bit of rain has just begun to fall. One kid runs to get a spot
near the front, and a pal saves a seat for her. When the bus door opens,
they hop in and find a dry seat at last. The trip to school is short, but
it goes past a farm, a shop, and a big oak tree. They talk and laugh the
whole way, glad the long wait is over.
