# Week 3 Math Practice

## Fractions
1. What is 1/2 + 1/4?
Answer: 3/4

2. Simplify 6/8 to lowest terms.
Answer: 3/4

## Decimals
3. Round 3.14159 to two decimal places.
Answer: 3.14 (±0.01)

## Shapes
4. Which shape has exactly three sides?
A) Square
B) Triangle
C) Hexagon
Answer: B

## Word Problems
5. Explain how you would find the perimeter of a rectangle that is 5 cm by 3 cm.
Answer: Add all four sides, or use 2×(length+width): 2×(5+3) = 16 cm.
