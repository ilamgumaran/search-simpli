# Week 3 ELA Practice

## Vocabulary
1. What is a synonym for "happy"?
Answer: joyful

2. What is an antonym for "brave"?
Answer: cowardly

## Grammar
3. Which word in this sentence is the verb: "The clever fox jumped over the fence."?
Answer: jumped

## Reading Response
4. Explain why the invented character Mira might have felt nervous on her first day at a new school.
Answer: A new school is unfamiliar, so a character might worry about making friends or not knowing the routine.

## Spelling
5. Spell the word that means "a large group of ships traveling together."
Answer: fleet
