# Week 3 Science Practice

## The Solar System
1. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
A) Venus
B) Mars
C) Jupiter
Answer: B

2. How many planets are in our solar system?
Answer: 8

## States of Matter
3. Name the three common states of matter.
Answer: solid, liquid, gas

4. Explain why an ice cube melts when it is left out on a warm day.
Answer: Heat energy from the room transfers into the ice, raising its temperature until it changes from solid to liquid.

## Invented Habitat Study
5. A made-up desert critter called a "sandhopper" only comes out at night. What is this behavior called?
Answer: nocturnal
