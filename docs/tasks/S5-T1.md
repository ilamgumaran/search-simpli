# S5-T1 · J2: the judged fixture that says where keyword search loses

**State:** ready (starts from `main` at `d05945d` or later) · **Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Depends on:** S3-T7 (the family-folder fixture and its frozen pack: the generator pattern, `relevance_smoke.py --pack`), S3-T2 (positions; `find` phrase and prefix), S4-T4 (file-name channel), `docs/research/2026-10-luxir-learnings.md` §3.0 (the seven slices J2 needs) · **Size:** M · **Series:** S5 search quality

**Owner:** the Xcode Mac orchestrator · **Orchestrator:** Claude Fable 5.1 · **Builder:** Claude Opus 5.5 (local; the content and the judgments need the stronger builder) · **Verifier:** Claude Opus 5.5 (local, a different agent)

**Why.** The S4 series made the engine fast; its quality gates are saturated. The family-folder pack's words recall rests on four cases and every gate reads 1.0; the relevance-smoke fixture is a mechanical invariant at 1.0; the mixed fixture's misses are paraphrases written for the vector channel. The engine has no stemming, no stop words, no title field, no proximity bonus in `search_knowledge`, no misspelling tolerance, and its file-name channel sees only the file stem. The research doc ruled in October that none of those is built before a harder judged fixture ("J2") shows, slice by slice, where keyword search loses and by how much; the S2-T6..T10 rows and the agreed order in `03-next.md` wait on it. The consumer's own accuracy pack (fake models) sits at 40/42 and 43/46 precision with two known causes (key words scattered across unrelated chunks; a follow-up read one chunk late), which J2's phrase slice and the app's two-step loop will take up later. S5-T1 builds J2 and its runner, records the baseline of today's engine on it through the shipped binary, and gives the series its regression gate. It changes no ranking.

**The rule carried from S4:** measured, not guessed. J2 is judged before any S5-T2+ build starts; every later quality change has a pre-registered bar on a J2 slice, a control slice with zero rank-1 losses allowed, and the old packs as no-regression checks.

## Rulings

1. **The fixture.** A new pack `fixtures/j2/` with its own `generate.py`, `content.py`, `README.md` and `judgments.json`, in the family-folder pattern (deterministic, standard-library only, every line range and sha256 computed from the files, `tests/test_j2_fixture.py` regenerates into a temporary folder and compares byte for byte). **Invented content only**: a made-up family, a made-up school and town, no real person, place or product name, nothing from any real folder (this repo is public). Three roots in the UC-007 shape (a school folder, a home folder, a laptop folder), at least 60 files (Markdown and plain text, a few with CRLF, a BOM, no final newline, one empty file; sizes from one line to about 400 lines; a Tamil file in each root), written in a kid's and a parent's phrasing. The content is written so that each slice below has its targets **and its distractors**: files that share some of the query's words without answering it. `fixtures/family-folder/**` is not touched: J2 is a second pack beside it, and the S3 gates keep running unchanged.
2. **The seven slices and their minimum sizes** (the research doc §3.0; a slice may be larger, never smaller):

   | Slice | Min queries | What the query and the content do |
   |---|---:|---|
   | **control** | 20 | exact words that appear in the answer; the engine of today should answer these (the no-loss slice for every later task) |
   | **misspelling** | 15 | the query misspells one word (one or two edits; at least five with a transposition); the file spells it right |
   | **morphology** | 12 | plural / singular, possessive, `-ing` / `-ed` / `-s` between query and file, both directions; at least three where the only matching word differs in form |
   | **accent / compatibility** | 6 | é, ï, ü, a full-width digit or letter, a ligature (ﬁ) between query and file, both directions |
   | **Tamil** | 10 | an inflected or case-suffixed form in the query, the base form in the file, and the reverse; plus two where a common suffix must not match an unrelated stem |
   | **title intent** | 10 | the answer is in the file named for the query (stem or folder name carries the words); the same words appear weakly in other files' bodies, so a body-only engine ranks them first |
   | **phrase / adjacency** | 8 | two or three words whose order or adjacency decides: the answer has them adjacent, a distractor has them far apart in one chunk (and one case the other way round) |

   Plus **negatives**: at least two queries per slice with no answer in the corpus (words that occur, in the wrong sense or the wrong file), recorded with an empty expected list; they are information rows in this task (the top hit's grade is reported), not gates.
3. **The judgments.** `judgments.json` v1 for J2: per query, `slice`, `id`, `question` (the kid's or parent's words), `query` (what is sent), `mode` (`knowledge` for `search_knowledge`, `find:words`, `find:phrase`, `find:prefix`) and a graded `expected` list of `{root_id, path, start_line, end_line, grade}` with grades **2 = the answer, 1 = useful context, 0 = named distractor** (the 0 rows make the distractor explicit for the reader; a hit that matches no row is grade 0 too). Hit matching as the family-folder pack defines it (root and path equal, line ranges overlap). **Judged before built**: the planner's plan lists every query with its graded targets and distractors in prose before `content.py` is written; the orchestrator rules on the plan; the generator then computes the line ranges. The judged pack is frozen on merge: a later change to a judgment is a reviewed commit that says why, as the family-folder README says.
4. **The runner and the metrics.** `scripts/j2_run.py` indexes `fixtures/j2/` and runs every query **through the shipped `searchd` binary** (`ReleaseSmall`, the same request surface the app uses: the knowledge query and `find`), and `relevance_smoke.py --pack j2` runs the same pack through the Python reference; both write a results file with, per slice and overall, **success@1** (grade 2 at rank 1), **MRR@10** (first hit of grade ≥ 1), **nDCG@10** (graded), **recall@5** (grade ≥ 1 found in the top 5), the per-query rank of the first grade-2 hit, and the negatives' top-hit grade. Where the two surfaces differ on a query, the Report names the query and says why (analyzer, chunking, tie order); a difference that is not explained is a finding, not noise. `top_k` 10, scope all three roots, file-name channel on (J2 is indexed with it, like the app's indexes since M16-T20).
5. **The baseline and the gate.** The runner on `main`'s engine at the branch point writes `fixtures/j2/baseline.json` (both surfaces, every metric per slice, the engine commit and the natives' digest). `scripts/j2_gate.py` compares a run against the baseline: **fails if any slice's MRR@10 drops by more than 0.02, if any control query loses rank 1, or if any query's first grade-2 rank worsens by more than 3 places without that query being named in the run's allow-list** (the allow-list is a file the task that moves a judgment commits beside it); otherwise it prints each slice's deltas. CI runs the Python-reference pack through the gate against the baseline (no Zig in CI by design; floors are the baseline, not 1.0). Plants for the verifier: (P1) BM25 `b` forced to 0 in a scratch build and (P2) case folding disabled in a scratch build must each fail the gate on at least one slice; (P3) a baseline file with one slice's MRR raised by 0.05 must fail the gate against an honest run; (P4) a judgment changed by hand without regenerating must fail `tests/test_j2_fixture.py`.
6. **Nothing else moves.** No ranking, analyzer, format or contract change; the family-folder pack, the relevance-smoke fixture, the mixed fixture, the Tamil bar and every suite run unchanged; the Python suite, `zig build test`, `test-abi`, the Dart tests and `check_requirements` green; CAP-11 / `docs/evaluation.md` gain a section naming J2, its slices, its metrics and the gate; `docs/journey/03-next.md` gets the S5 entry (the baseline table: where keyword search loses today, slice by slice) and the S2-T6..T10 / "agreed order" rows are updated to point at J2's numbers; `PROJECT-STATE.md` entry by the verifier.
7. **Diff scope.** `fixtures/j2/**` (new), `scripts/j2_run.py`, `scripts/j2_gate.py` (new), `relevance_smoke.py` (the `j2` pack registered; no change to the existing packs' numbers), `tests/test_j2_fixture.py`, `tests/test_j2_gate.py` (new), `.github/workflows/checks.yml` (one step), `docs/requirements/cap-11-*.md` or `docs/evaluation.md` (a section), `docs/journey/03-next.md`, `docs/tasks/S5-T1.md`, `PROJECT-STATE.md`.
