**What is different here.** The rule runs at query time over the opened index's own words, so it may look at what the files hold. Phase 1 decides between them on rows judged before the rule exists.

2. **The forms (candidate rule R).** For an analysed query word *t* of 4 to 49 letters a–z, after fold-groups-v1's steps F and A:
   - **Bases.** *t* itself, and by ending:
     - `-ing` with a remainder *r* of 3 or more letters holding a vowel (`a e i o u y`): *r*, *r* + `e`, and *r* with its doubled final consonant undoubled when it ends in one (not `l`, `s`, `z` doubled, which stay as *r* too);
     - `-ed` with a remainder *r* of 3 or more letters holding a vowel: *r*, *r* + `e`, the undoubled *r* as above;
     - `-ied` with 5 or more letters: the remainder + `y`.
     - The plural step S of fold-groups-v1 is applied to *t* first, so `plants` and `planting` share the base `plant`.
   - **Relatives of a base *b*.** *b*, *b* + `s`, *b* + `es`, *b* + `ed`, *b* + `d` when *b* ends in `e`, *b* + `ing`, *b* less its final `e` + `ing`, *b* + its final consonant + `ed` and + `ing` when *b* ends consonant-vowel-consonant, and for a *b* ending in a consonant + `y`: the `-ies` and `-ied` forms.

#### R as prototyped (ruling 2)

The record states R exactly; in short:
