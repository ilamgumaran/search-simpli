## Source

**Why.** At `4afd4c3` word forms are J2's largest measured loss. `searchd` `content` success@1: morphology **0.538** (13 rows), held-out morphology **0.571** (14 rows). fold-groups-v1 groups plurals only. The rows it leaves:
- `o05` `planting tulips` (the answer says `planted`), `o06` `football practice cancelled` (`cancelling`), `o11` `skip rope` (`skipping`), `o12` `painted pots` (`paint`);
- held-out `oh11` `knitting` (`knitted`), `oh12` `cycled` (`cycling`), `oh13` `danced maypole` (`dancing`).

**What S5-T4 measured and rejected** (index side and key-based, every member at weight 1.0):
- Porter's `-ed` with undoubling: control `c22` `tomato seedlings potted` 1 → 5 (`potted` joins `pot`).
- `-ed` with the doubled form kept, plus `-ing`: the gate passes and morphology reaches 0.692, but `o07` goes 1 → 2, `o05` drops to 6, the mixed pack loses a row, and a keep list cannot be complete (`outing` / `out`, `dressing` / `dress`, `pasted` / `past`).

**What is different here.** The rule runs at query time over the opened index's own words, so it may look at what the files hold. Two restraints that an analyzer cannot have are measured before anything ships: a lower weight for a verb-form relative, and expanding only a word the files do not hold. Phase 1 decides between them on rows judged before the rule exists.

## Rulings

1. **Step 0, the regrade pass, is its own commit and comes first.** Read S5-T3's ruling Q3 and the 03-next row. Grade the H1/intro chunks of `m03`, `m04`, `m15` and `m16` as the row says, re-record every baseline cell with `--why` naming this task, and list every cell that moves and every cell that does not. If success@1 cannot move by its definition (first grade-2 rank), say so in the Report rather than changing the definition; the grade the row names is the one applied. No engine source changes in this commit.

2. **The forms (candidate rule R).** For an analysed query word *t* of 4 to 49 letters a–z, after fold-groups-v1's steps F and A:
   - **Bases.** *t* itself, and by ending:
     - `-ing` with a remainder *r* of 3 or more letters holding a vowel (`a e i o u y`): *r*, *r* + `e`, and *r* with its doubled final consonant undoubled when it ends in one (not `l`, `s`, `z` doubled, which stay as *r* too);
     - `-ed` with a remainder *r* of 3 or more letters holding a vowel: *r*, *r* + `e`, the undoubled *r* as above;
     - `-ied` with 5 or more letters: the remainder + `y`.
     - The plural step S of fold-groups-v1 is applied to *t* first, so `plants` and `planting` share the base `plant`.
   - **Relatives of a base *b*.** *b*, *b* + `s`, *b* + `es`, *b* + `ed`, *b* + `d` when *b* ends in `e`, *b* + `ing`, *b* less its final `e` + `ing`, *b* + its final consonant + `ed` and + `ing` when *b* ends consonant-vowel-consonant, and for a *b* ending in a consonant + `y`: the `-ies` and `-ied` forms.
   - **Members.** A relative is a member only if the opened index holds it as a body term some chunk in scope holds, or as a title term of a visible file under the prefix. This is fold-groups-v1's visibility rule unchanged: a word that only hidden chunks or files hold is never searched and never named.
   - The builder may restate R more exactly where this text is ambiguous, in the Phase 1 record, before any held-out run. Lookup by generated form or by a key is the builder's choice, provided the Zig engine and the Python mirror are held equal by a golden.

3. **The candidates Phase 1 measures.** Each on the Python mirror with the engine's simple body fold (the base that reproduces `searchd`, S5-T4's trap), under `content` and `both`:
   - **A.** R's members join the word's fold group at weight 1.0. The reference; expected to fail control.
   - **B.** R's members join at weight 0.5 (fuzzy-v1's lower weight); the word itself and its plural group stay at 1.0.
   - **C.** R applies only to a word whose fold-groups-v1 group is empty in scope (the files hold neither the word nor a plural form of it). Members weigh 1.0. Such a word is not also expanded by fuzzy-v1 when R gives it a member.
   - **D.** C's members at 1.0 for a word the files do not hold; B's members at 0.5 for a word they do.
   - The builder may add up to two candidates of its own, declared in the record before any held-out run.
   - A keep list is allowed only as a short, named list in the rule document, each word with the pair it guards. It is not allowed to be the thing that makes a candidate pass control.

4. **Held-out rows first.** Before any code of R exists on either surface, one commit adds a slice `verbform-heldout` to J2: at least 14 rows, in new files only, judged, and recorded with today's engine.
   - At least 8 rows where the answer holds only another verb form: `-ing` ↔ `-ed`, base ↔ `-ing`, base ↔ `-ed`, with a doubled consonant, a dropped `e` and a `-ied` among them.
   - At least 4 guard rows where a look-alike must not win: a word ending in `-ing` or `-ed` whose remainder is another word the fixture holds in a grade-0 chunk (`evening` / `even`, `outing` / `out`, `pasted` / `past`, `wedding` / `wed` are the kind; the rows are the builder's, the content invented).
   - At least 2 rows where the typed word is held by a grade-0 chunk and the answer holds another form (the case candidate C cannot reach and B can).
   - No query word or form of the morphology or held-out morphology slices is reused. The generator asserts it. `tests/test_j2_fixture.py` stays green.

5. **The bars a candidate must meet to ship** (judged on `searchd` in Phase 2; on the mirror in Phase 1):
