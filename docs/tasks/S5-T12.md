## Source

`Engine.bothGroups` builds a visible-collection view before it knows whether any query word needs one. The queue records a warm `both` query with no triggering word (`bazecha`, 10k timing corpus) at about 0.053–0.059 → 0.085–0.090 ms, and a grouped frequent word (`system`) missing its `both` increment bound (+0.065 ms against +0.03). Both are earlier whole-query measurements. The eager view is seen in the source; that it causes the cost is a hypothesis until measured.

**What reproduces.**

1. **The `bazecha` overhead reproduces, and it is the eager view.**
   - Today's `both` `bazecha` is 0.1383 ms. The queue's 0.053–0.059 → 0.085–0.090 was S5-T3's engine at `top_k` 5,
     before the title field's and fold groups' later work, so the absolute figures are not comparable.
   - The increment is what reproduces: S5-T3 recorded +0.032 ms for the fuzzy work on this query. Skipping the view
     when no word triggers removes **0.0281 ms** [0.0269, 0.0300], against a noise band of at most 0.0022 in any
     round.
2. **It is not `bazecha`'s alone: every `both` query on this corpus pays it.**
   - The no-group queries (`benico`, both orders of `hybrid retrieval`) save 0.028–0.031 ms under `proto`.
   - The grouped ones save as much under `noview` (`system` −0.0310, `hashing` −0.0300, `system systems` −0.0309,
     `systems system` −0.0305).
   - The view costs the same whatever the query: it is a pass over the scope, not over the query's postings.
3. **Why: the work finds nothing to hide.**
   - `ss_query` with `search_field` `both` always passes a root mask (`abi.zig`, from `folder.allowedRoots`), and the
     handle's engine has no statistics groups loaded.
   - So `scope.visibleCollection` walks all 19,959 chunks on every `both` query. Every chunk is visible here, so it
     returns null, and the walk is thrown away.
   - `content` without `roots` passes no mask and never walks (its `proto` and `noview` columns are flat).
   - `filename_field.maskHides` already answers the same question for the filename channel from the mask alone.
   - `visibleCollection` has no such check: it returns early only when there is no mask.
4. **`proto` costs grouped queries nothing.** Its `both` grouped figures are within noise of `main` (−0.0004 to
   +0.0009), as designed: one grouping without the view, then the view as before, with no recompute because the view hides
   nothing here. No query in the set is slower beyond
   noise under either build; the largest `content` median is +0.0009, `noview` `system`.
5. **Term order no longer matters.** `system systems` and `systems system` are 0.2831 / 0.2833 under `both` and
   0.1255 / 0.1257 under `content`. S5-T4 measured 0.17 / 0.25 for the plain query before fold groups; now both
   words fall in the one group, which the body walk scores once.
6. **The grouped word's `both` remainder is small, and is not the view.** With the view gone (`noview`), `both` −
   `content` is +0.093 (`benico`), +0.099 (`bazecha`) and +0.106 (`hybrid retrieval`) with no group, against
   +0.112 (`hashing`), +0.116 (`system`) and +0.126 (the two-word grouped query).
   - So `filename_field`'s walk of a group's touched chunks adds about 0.01–0.02 ms over a no-group word's `both`

**1. Part 1: the check, and its exact condition.**

- **The condition.** `scope.visibleCollection` returns null ("everything visible") without walking the chunks when
  `labeled_documents` is known false **and** either there is no mask, or the mask allows every root of every document.
  The second half is `RootMask.allowsEvery(documents.len)`: the mask is marked `rooted` (set only by `Folder.mask`, and
  only when every document of the folder has a root and the mask covers every root), it has an entry for every
  document, and every root is allowed.
- **Why exactly this is the walk's "everything visible".** With no labelled record, `documentVisible` reduces to the
  mask. A rooted mask that allows every root allows every document, so the walk would count `documents.len` and return
  null. In every other case the walk runs as before and may still return null. Two cases are not short-cut, because the
  walk's answer could differ: a document in no root (`no_root`, which the mask hides), and a mask not built by
  `Folder.mask`.
- **The proof by test** (`zig/src/both_view_test.zig`, test 1). In each case the check's answer is compared with a full
  walk's: every root allowed; a labelled record under an all-roots mask, with and without its label; one root hidden; a
  document in no root; a hand-made mask; a short mask. Test 2 goes through the engine: an unscoped `both` or `content`
  query walks 0 times, and with a labelled record it walks once.
- **Measured alone** (`part1` − `main`, two acceptance runs, 2.4): every unscoped `both` query −0.0306 to −0.0337 ms;
  `both` on the two-root index with every root allowed −0.0307 to −0.0341. Under a mask that hides a root, part 1
  changes nothing (−0.0018 to +0.0028; there the walk is needed). `content` −0.0001 to +0.0011.

**2. Part 2: the lazy view.**

2.1 **A correction to Phase 1.** Phase 1 said a group's existence never reads the view. That is wrong under a hiding
scope. `fold.appendVerbForms` admits a held word's verb form only when the form's key df is no higher than the word's,
and under a scope that df is the scope's. The Phase 1 statement held on the Phase 1 corpus only because nothing there
was hidden. `8536159`'s message records the correction. So the view is built when a key df is read: a kept group's
members, or a verb form compared with a held word. That is slightly broader than ruling 2's "only when a group exists"
(deviation 2).

2.2 **What was built.** `scope.LazyView` holds the request (mask, labels, statistics) and builds the view at its first
`get()`, at most once. `fuzzy.Context.keyFrequency` uses it when no eager view is set. `Engine.bothGroups` passes one,
and `content` keeps its eager view. `fold.compute` no longer reads a member's key df in `member()`: it collects the
members with their body entries, discards as before, and reads the key dfs (`Collected.keyFrequencies`) only for a kept
group.

- **Walk counts** (test 3). Under a mask hiding one root, queries with no group walk 0 times (`shelf`, `toys`,
  `zebra`, `shelf toys`). Grouped ones walk 1 (`box`, `box boxes shelf`). `content` walks 1, as before.
- **The view is the request's** (test 4, on `skewed_docs`).
  - `cafes` orders its group [café, cafe] unscoped and [cafe, café] under the mask, exactly as the `content` path's
    eager view orders it.
  - `paint` gives no group unscoped and [painting] under the mask, again matching `content`.
