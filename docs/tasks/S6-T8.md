## Source

After step 2 the 10k timing index is 2.17 times its corpus. The highlight offsets `find` reads (per token, where it starts and how long it is, in codepoints) are stored as varints. [`docs/index-size.md`](../index-size.md) § C5b settles the design: per token one byte, two 4-bit fields (the zigzag gap in codepoints between the previous token's end and this token's start; the token's length), a field value of 15 escaping to a varint after the byte; the token count per document as a varint. Measured by the S6-T3 prototype: −1,195,645 B (timing 10k), −3,229,866 B (prose 10k), −1,198,396 B (Tamil 10k). Positions stay mandatory and the offsets stay (C5 and C6 were ruled out on 16 Nov); what `find` returns does not change.

### What was built

- **Format** ([`lexical-segment-format-v4.md`](../lexical-segment-format-v4.md)). The offsets live in the lexical
  segment, so by the repository's convention this is a new lexical version: `HYBLEX04`, v3 with section 5 recoded
  as C5b. Per document a varint token count, then per token one byte: low nibble `min(zigzag(gap), 15)`, high
  nibble `min(length, 15)`, the gap measured from the previous token's end (0 at the document's start); a nibble
  of 15 is followed by that field's whole value as a varint, the gap's first. Header flags exactly `0x0003`. Twelve
  validity rules, each with its own error; a worked example of four documents (16 bytes; v2 takes 32) with an
  escape in each field and both at once. Only the **content** section is v4; the file-name section stays
  `HYBLEX03`. Manifest v2.5: flag bit 7 `lexical_v4`, only with bit 5.
- **Ruling 1's stop conditions:** none met. Contracts and `zig/include/search_simpli.h` are unchanged; the saving
  equals S6-T3's figure to the byte; the design was built as written.
- **Negative gaps (ruling 7) happen.** NFC changes a region as a whole and the offset map gives every normalized
  codepoint of the region its whole span (`analysis.zig`), so two tokens in one region overlap: `a` U+037E U+2126
  gives gap −3; `a`, six U+037E, U+2126 gives −8, zigzag 15, the escape boundary. The writer keeps each start at
  or after the previous start and each length at least 1 (`postings.appendOffsetsTable`, as `buildPositional`
  did), so a gap is never below minus the previous token's length. Zero-token documents exist (a chunk of
  punctuation only).
- **Code** (core only): `postings.zig` (the coding, `validateOneByteOffsets`, readers of both codings,
  `withOffsetsCoding`; since `e5d1734` also `oneByteTableEnd` and `transcodePairsTable`), `lexical_segment.zig`
  (magic, version 4, header, decode, encode), `lexical_build.zig` and `apply.zig` (writers; `mergeCarried`
  transcodes a previous generation's tables when the coding differs), `manifest.zig` (bit 7), `find.zig` (rule 11 at
  a hit's highlights), `abi.zig` (the new errors are `SS_ERR_CORRUPT_SNAPSHOT`). Validation of section 5 stays lazy,
  at the first `find`, as in v3. `check_layers.py`: OK, 45 files, 6 allowed interface imports, as `main`.
- **Rule 11 where hits are made (`fb74582`, made after measuring).** `ab42899` checked "no offset past the text" also
  in `find`'s `deduplicate`, which counted every candidate chunk's codepoints; `main` never did, and my first two
  timing runs put five fixtures `find` cells beyond the controls by T (+24 to +129 µs, high-hit words).
  `deduplicate` reads offsets only as line numbers (`lineOf` stops at the text's end), so it now skips the check;
  `makeHit` still refuses a returned hit's highlight past the text (`OffsetsPastText`, tested through `ops.find`).
- **The writer's two changes of item 1b (`e5d1734`, made after measuring; the rework, item 1, below).** Finding where
  each carried document's v4 table starts (`apply.offsetTableStarts`) now steps 16 tokens at a time when none of
  their bytes holds an escape nibble (`postings.oneByteTableEnd`), and the first publish's transcode of a v2 table to
  v4 (`postings.transcodePairsTable`) writes the bytes in one pass into reserved space instead of reading the table
  into a list of pairs and encoding it again. Neither changes a byte the writer writes.
