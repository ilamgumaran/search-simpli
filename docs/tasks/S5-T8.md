## Source

J2's 95 answerable questions sit at 0.832 right-file-first, and the rules that got them there also return hits for questions nothing answers. `painting the fence` returns ten hits, the first a model-making file reached through `paint` and through `the` in a file name. `skating` reaches `saving`, `setting`, `sorting`. `hopping` can generate `hopes`; `evening` can generate `even`. None of this is measured as a rate: J2 holds a handful of negatives, recorded as information and gated by nothing. This task first makes irrelevant hits measurable, then tries three narrow rules one at a time, and ships only what the measurement supports.

## Rulings

1. **Phase 1a, the measure, before any rule.** One commit, with today's engine's results recorded:
   - **Negatives.** At least 30 new unanswerable questions in two named sets: `negatives-tuning` (rules may be shaped on it) and `negatives-heldout` (at least 15, never looked at through a candidate until every candidate is declared). They cover: a common word in a file name (`the`, `and`, `of`, `my`); a correctly spelt word no file holds that has near words; a topic near a held topic; a verb form whose remainder is another held word; Tamil and accented questions; a two-word question where one word is held and the topic is not.
   - **What counts as misleading.** Written in `fixtures/j2/README.md` before any run: for an unanswerable question, each hit in the top 5 is labelled by what brought it (a stop word alone; a near spelling; a verb form of another word; a real word of the question in an unrelated passage). The measure per set is the mean number of top-5 hits, and the share of questions whose first hit came through a stop word alone, a near spelling or a cross-word form. A hit that holds a real word of the question is counted separately and is not a target of this task. No score threshold and no answer-confidence value is defined (review R06 Exit).
   - **R05's rows.** Opposing `hop` / `hope` and `evening` / `even` documents with judged questions on both sides, the answers spread over at least six files so that `both` measures what `content` does.
   - **Controls.** At least 8 new answerable rows in a `precision-control` slice that a too-eager rule would break: an answer reached only through a stop-word-heavy title, only through a near spelling, only through a verb form.
   - The generator asserts no reuse of an earlier slice's query words. `tests/test_j2_fixture.py` stays green. The baseline gains the new sets through `--write-baseline --why`; no earlier cell moves.
2. **Phase 1b, three candidates, one at a time**, on the Python reference, each declared in the prototype record before its held-out run:
   - **W, query stop words in the title part.** A short fixed English list (the builder proposes it from Lucene's 33 words; no Tamil list in this task). A stop word does not score against the title field and does not count in title coverage; the body is unchanged. Then, separately, **Wb**: the same words dropped from the body only when the question holds at least one other word.
   - **F, a floor for fuzzy-v1.** A near word is kept only if it passes an eligibility test the builder proposes and measures: at least a floor on the member's document frequency, or a tighter edit rule for a correctly shaped word, or both. The held-out misspelling slice is the guard.
   - **X, cross-word verb forms.** A narrow eligibility rule in R for the `e` shapes and the look-alike remainders, tested beside the existing cap and membership rule. The verb-form feature is not disabled.
   - Each alone, then the passing ones together.
3. **The bars for a candidate to ship.** (a) control and `precision-control` success@1 1.000 in `content` and `both`; (b) the S5-T6 gate clean, the allow-list untouched; (c) no slice loses a rank-1 answer, and misspelling-heldout, morphology-heldout and verbform-heldout no lower in any cell; (d) on `negatives-heldout`, the share of questions whose first hit is misleading falls, and the mean top-5 hit count does not rise; (e) the old packs identical or each move named; (f) every moved first grade-2 rank named. **A candidate that meets (a)–(c) and does not move (d) does not ship.** Shipping none is an acceptable result, with the measure and the rows kept.
4. **Phase 1 stops for the orchestrator** with the per-candidate table, the bars, the cost count on the 10k timing corpus, and the builder's recommendation, including the policy ids and contract fields each shipped candidate would change.
5. **Phase 2, the engine**, for the ruled candidates only: Zig equal to the reference by goldens (which the S5-T6 job then checks); changed matching is a new policy id for each rule it touches and a contracts minor version, named in the Phase 1 rulings; Dart follows; natives rebuilt and stamped; `find` stays exact and the `filename` field unchanged; timing bounds restated at the Phase 1 rulings; plants; the baseline re-recorded on both surfaces; the branch's CI run green, native J2 included; documents; the Report ending with "What M16 carries".
